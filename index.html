Key Risks

R1 — AI output not good enough to send

Existential
If users don't trust Co-Pilot drafts enough to send them, the Day-9 milestone collapses and the entire activation model fails.
Mitigation: Wizard-of-oz test pre-build with 10–15 users. Hard quality gate: Co-Pilot Send Rate ≥ 40% before public launch. Tone variants + "explain why" function build trust.
Discovery: A1 assumption · P-04 ▾

R2 — Async cohorts don't replicate coach accountability

Existential
5-person Slack cohorts may generate low participation, feel hollow, and fail to carry the accountability load a coach would have provided.
Mitigation: Structured prompts are non-negotiable. 7-day silence detection → team intervention. Cohort Participation Rate ≥ 40% gate. Quantitative correlation test at Day 60.
Discovery: A2 assumption · Theme 6 · H8 ▾

R3 — Content lock drives exits not action

High
Locking Co-Pilot and community behind Day-1 action is perceived as coercive, not enabling. Users who want to explore first may leave.
Mitigation: Copy tone is critical — "unlock" not "locked out." Preview one peer story pre-Day-1. Show exactly what unlocks. Monitor Day-1 Action Rate from day one of beta.
Discovery: A4 assumption · P-21, P-24 ▾

R4 — Peer Gallery empty at launch

High
The gallery that does the trust work is empty at launch. Users see a blank gallery and conclude the product is unproven.
Mitigation: 20–30 beta users recruited 60–90 days before public launch. Seed gallery with their verified stories. Transparent "Early user" label. Target ≥5 verified stories at launch.
Discovery: Cold start · P-20 · P-26 ▾

R5 — User stalls at Day 5 with no responses

Medium
The Day-5 branch logic detects a stall, but the user doesn't trust the reoriented plan enough to continue. Confidence drops.
Mitigation: Day-4 card explicitly sets expectations ("normal to have no responses"). Branch B must feel like a real pivot. Cohort prompt surfaces peer stories. 48h nudge email fires.
Discovery: P-22 dropout pattern · Day-9 cliff ▾
